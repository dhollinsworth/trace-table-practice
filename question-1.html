Why are there no A or B grades?

The first decision tests Score ≥ 40. A Yes immediately assigns C and goes to the output. A No means the score is below 40, so it cannot meet either later condition (≥ 60 or ≥ 80). Therefore, this flowchart gives C for scores of 40 or more and D for scores below 40.
